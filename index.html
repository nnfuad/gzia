7th President of Bangladesh • The architect of modern nationalism & economic revival

Military hero, statesman, and founder of BNP. He declared independence in 1971, reconstructed Bangladesh after turmoil, and introduced the historic 19-point program for development.

Biography

Ziaur Rahman (19 January 1936 – 30 May 1981) was a Bangladeshi military officer, freedom fighter, and politician. He served as the 7th President of Bangladesh from 1977 to 1981. As a sector commander during the Liberation War of 1971, he famously declared the independence of Bangladesh from Kalurghat Radio Station. After the assassination of Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman in 1975 and subsequent coups, Zia emerged as the strongman and later assumed presidency following a referendum. He introduced multi-party democracy, founded the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP) in 1978, and implemented sweeping economic reforms.

His leadership focused on food production, population control, rural development, and forging strong diplomatic ties with Muslim nations & SAARC. He was awarded Bir Uttom, Bangladesh's second-highest military honor. His legacy remains a cornerstone of Bangladeshi nationalism.

Path to Presidency: Revolution & Transition

After the brutal assassination of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman in 1975, a series of military coups destabilized the nation. Major General Ziaur Rahman, then Chief of Army Staff, gradually consolidated power amidst political chaos. In November 1975, he became the Deputy Chief Martial Law Administrator, and by 1977, following a national referendum with overwhelming support, he assumed the presidency. His "Revolution of 1977" restored order, lifted martial law partially, and set Bangladesh on a democratic path. He later reintroduced parliamentary elections, bringing political stability.

19-Point Movement

In 1979, President Ziaur Rahman launched the historic 19-point program—an ambitious socio-economic reform agenda. It focused on: food self-sufficiency, rural development, population control, expansion of primary education, boosting agriculture & irrigation, industrialization, women empowerment, combating corruption, and strengthening local government. This blueprint accelerated Bangladesh's growth and became the foundation of his political manifesto. The 19 Points electrified the rural masses and modernized the economy.

Milestones & Nation Building

The Historic 19-Point Programme

President Ziaur Rahman introduced the historic 19-point programme as a comprehensive blueprint for socio-economic development, self-reliance, democracy, justice, and national unity in Bangladesh.

1. To protect the independence, integrity and sovereignty of the country at all costs.

2. The four fundamental principles of the constitution — complete faith and trust in Almighty Allah, democracy, nationalism, and socialism of social and economic justice — are to be reflected in all aspects of national life.

3. To build ourselves into a self-reliant nation by all means.

4. Ensuring public participation in all levels of administration, development activities and maintenance of law and order.

5. Strengthening the rural and national economy through agricultural development on the basis of highest priority.

6. To make the country self-sufficient in food and ensure that no one goes hungry.

7. To increase the production of cloth in the country and ensure the supply of at least thick cloth for everyone.

8. To make every possible effort to ensure that no citizen is left homeless.

9. To free the country from the curse of illiteracy.

10. To provide minimum medical care for all citizens.

11. To establish the due status of women in society and to motivate the youth to build the nation by uniting them.

12. Providing necessary encouragement to the private sector for the economic development of the country.

13. To develop healthy labor-owner relations in the interest of improving the conditions of workers and increasing production.

14. To encourage public service and nation-building among government employees and to improve their financial condition.

15. Preventing population explosion.

16. To build friendship with all foreign countries on the basis of equality and to strengthen relations with Muslim countries.

17. Decentralize administration and development systems and strengthen local governments.

18. Establish a corruption-free, justice-based social system.

19. To fully protect the rights of all citizens, regardless of religion, caste, or tribe, and to strengthen national unity and solidarity.

“Self-reliance, discipline, production, and national unity formed the foundation of Ziaur Rahman’s development philosophy.”

Begum Khaleda Zia

Wife of President Zia, former Prime Minister of Bangladesh (1991–1996, 2001–2006). First female PM of Bangladesh, iconic BNP leader. Passed away in December 2025.

Tarique Rahman

Son of Ziaur Rahman & Khaleda Zia. Senior Vice Chairman of BNP, influential political figure and Current PM of Bangladesh.
