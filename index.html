Ziaur Rahman
Bir Uttom

7th President of Bangladesh • The Leader of the Nation

A visionary statesman, military leader, and founder of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP). President Ziaur Rahman steered Bangladesh towards economic resilience, national unity, and international recognition. His legacy of courage and development continues to inspire millions.

Enduring Legacy

Economic Revival

Initiated industrialization & food production reforms, turning Bangladesh into a developing economy during the late 70s.

Foreign Diplomacy

Strengthened ties with Muslim nations & SAARC founding member. Elevated Bangladesh's global standing.

Nationalist Vision

Founded BNP, introduced multiparty democracy, and promoted Bangladeshi nationalism over regionalism.

Bir Uttom

Hero of Liberation War 1971, declared Independence on behalf of Bangabandhu, recipient of highest gallantry award.

Historical Milestones
1971

Declaration of Independence

On 27 March, Ziaur Rahman declared the independence of Bangladesh from Kalurghat radio station, inspiring the nation.

1977

Became President

Took office after national referendum, initiating a period of political stability and economic reconstruction.

1978

Formation of BNP

Founded Bangladesh Nationalist Party, introducing multi-party democracy after years of military rule.

1981

Assassination & Legacy

President Zia was martyred on 30 May 1981, but his ideals of development, nationalism and democracy persist.
